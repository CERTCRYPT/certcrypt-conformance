# CHANGELOG

This contract follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html);
see [Versioning](README.md#versioning). The active version is in [`VERSION`](VERSION).

Entries are one line per change: `type:scope: what changed`, where `type` is
`feature`, `bugfix`, `breaking`, `deprecation`, `security` or `docs`.

## 1.0.0 — initial contract

Released contract versions are immutable.

* feature:Vectors: 160 conformance fixtures — 23 positive (`P001`–`P016`, `F001`, `F005`, `S001`, `S006`, `Q001`, `Q002`, `C001`) and 137 adversarial (`V001`–`V083`, `F002`–`F004`, `F006`–`F018`, `S002`–`S005`, `S007`–`S011`, `Q003`–`Q005`, `QN1`–`QN8`, `C002`–`C019`), each pinning the `result_code`, the resolved `verification_target` and the exact set of `failed_check_ids` a conformant verifier must return. The prefix names the dimension under test (`P` end-to-end paths, `V` verification-path violations, `F` the `system-foundation/1` statement, `S` signature authenticity, `Q` the post-quantum composite, `QN` quorum counting, `C` the CCSP/1 certificate envelope); the directory names the polarity. The adversarial half is the load-bearing one — a verifier returning `*_VALID` for everything passes no negative vector, and the negatives are what pin fail-closed behaviour: stripped post-quantum component, substituted capacity key, equivocating quorum, mis-domained signature
* feature:Primitives: `composite-verification` pins the refusals a composite verifier makes around the cryptography — missing post-quantum or classical component, profile mismatch, mode other than AND, unknown component algorithm, tampered component, components out of order or repeated, `encoding` other than `raw-rs-64`, `low_s` false or absent, undefined members in a component or in the object, and a classical key in any encoding but the one its field fixes (compressed SEC1, SPKI, a raw 32-byte key under the P-256 profile)
* feature:Vectors: Every signature in every fixture is real and is verified: a verifier has no mode that accepts a signature without verifying it, and the suite gives it none to rely on
* feature:Vectors: the Proof of Independence of `independence.proof/1` — every proof carries the mandatory composite signature by the system-domain Level-1 capacity key over its signed view; `S008` drops it and expects `INCOMPLETE` (`PROOF_SIGNATURE_VALID`); `V053` signs a `proof_id` that is not the digest of the identity view and `V054` omits `closure_time` (`REJECTED`, `PROOF_STRUCTURE_VALID`); `V055` signs an `anti_replay` derived for another settlement context (`INDEPENDENT_INVALID`, `NON_REPLAY_VALID`); `V056` carries a finality witness that disagrees with the settlement material (`REJECTED`, `FINALITY_SUFFICIENT`); `P010` carries a node attestation and pins the `attested_node` the verifier reports, and `S007` alters it after signing (`INDEPENDENT_INVALID`, `PROOF_SIGNATURE_VALID`)
* feature:Vectors: settlement under `settlement.aggregated.xrpl/1` — the burned quantity is checked against the total anchored by the batch binding memo (`batch_binding_preimage.total_quantity`), never the unanchored `batch_manifest` (`V043`, `V044`); every member the manifest shares with the anchored preimage must repeat it (`V046` leaf count, `V047` batch id); a missing manifest is `INCOMPLETE` (`V045`); the settlement action must be a `payment` from the aggregate pool with `action_ordinal` `0`, also when the signed reference names the same other value (`V048`–`V050`, `V058`); the tree width is the anchored `leaf_count`, not the proof's `batch.size` (`V059`); the quantity is a strictly positive amount with exactly two fractional digits (`V060`–`V062`); irreversibility must not trail the inclusion ledger (`V051`) and the inclusion ledger's hash must be present (`V063`)
* feature:Vectors: the successor-binding quorum of `system-manifest/1` §12.3 — an observation counts only when at least `k` distinct members of the predecessor entry sign its §9 payload with verifying composite signatures (`P009` valid; `QN5` k-1 signatures, `QN6` non-member, `QN7` tampered member signature, `QN8` one member signing twice); two authenticated observations over one predecessor state are equivocation (`QN4`, §12.5)
* feature:Vectors: the CCSP/1 envelope — `C001` carries `metadata`, `extensions`, an issuer `kid` and a published `payload.anchor_canon_digest_hex`, with repeated array elements, and reaches `INDEPENDENT_VALID` (the emission binding covers the whole certificate); `C002`–`C016` each break one envelope rule on a correctly signed certificate and expect `REJECTED` (`BASE_CERTIFICATE_STRUCTURE_VALID`): reserved `payload.consensus`, `payload.economics`, `payload.anchor.claim`, `payload.anchor.economic_policy_id`; a top-level, anchor, issuer-signature or signer member outside the closed set; `issued_at` with an offset; `root_id` not hex; two issuer signatures; a published anchor or payload digest that does not recompute; another system domain; components out of order. `C017` names a rules version the verifier does not evaluate (`UNSUPPORTED`). `C018` repeats `issued_at` inside the anchor with an escaped spelling and `C019` repeats a member inside `metadata` (`REJECTED`, `INPUT_COMBINATION_VALID`); `V015` repeats a top-level member of a current certificate
* feature:Primitives: `strict-json` pins which JSON text parses — no object repeats a member name at any depth, compared after escape decoding; repeated array elements are data — and which JSON subject materials (`subject.ai-decision/1`, `subject.collection/1`, `subject.context-snapshot/1`) are refused before canonicalization
* feature:Schemas: `base-certificate` — the shape of a CCSP/1 certificate (closed top level, anchor, issuer signature, signer and components; reserved names; formats). Every positive certificate validates and every C-series fixture whose defect is a shape fails it; the rules a schema cannot express are listed in its `$comment`
* feature:Vectors: `Q001` and `Q003` pin `additional_verifications.pqc_base_certificate_authentic` (`performed`, `algorithm`, `passed`; `reason` left free), so a verifier that fails the ML-DSA-65 component but reports the slot as `not_attempted` does not pass
* feature:Vectors: the authorization window of `capacity.evidence/1` §7 is inclusive at both ends — `P012` issues the commitment at `valid_from` exactly and is valid; `V064` issues it one second earlier (`INDEPENDENT_INVALID`, `CAPACITY_EVIDENCE_VALID`)
* feature:Vectors: both routes and the package — `P013` verifies a certificate against its material with target `issued` (`ISSUED_VALID`) and `V065` with material that is not the committed one (`ISSUED_INVALID`, `ORIGINAL_MATERIAL_COMPARISON_VALID`; a material mismatch is a failure of the Issued route, also in `V039` and `V057`); `P016` verifies an equivalent `subject.email/1` serialization and `V082` rejects a transit-added header; `V083` fixes an artifact set with Independent-route material but no base certificate as `INCOMPLETE` under `BASE_CERTIFICATE_PRESENT`; `P014` and `P015` supply a `verification_package` (Independent and Issued); `V069` adds `original_material` and `V070` omits `verification_context` (`REJECTED`, `PACKAGE_STRUCTURE_VALID`); `V071` declares another rules version and `V072` another `settlement_reference` (`REJECTED`, `PACKAGE_CONTEXT_COHERENT`); `V073` substitutes the certificate of another leaf (`INDEPENDENT_INVALID`, `LEAF_BINDING_VALID`)
* feature:Vectors: fixed-value members — `V075` carries an `independence_result` other than `independent` (`REJECTED`, `PROOF_STRUCTURE_VALID`); `V076` is evidence whose Level-1 authorization, honestly signed, grants a role other than `capacity_commitment_holder` (`INDEPENDENT_INVALID`, `CAPACITY_EVIDENCE_VALID`)
* feature:Vectors: closed capacity evidence — `V077` omits `projected_commitment`, `V078` uses a non-canonical operation salt, `V079` uses a non-canonical commitment instant, `V080` adds an undefined top-level member and `V081` adds one inside `level1_binding`; all are refused without throwing (`INDEPENDENT_INVALID`, `CAPACITY_EVIDENCE_VALID`)
* feature:Vectors: target selection and material delivery — `V068` asks for `auto` with capacity evidence and an inclusion proof but no proof (`INCOMPLETE`, target `Independent`); `V066` delivers material for a subject index the certificate does not declare, `V074` delivers an opaque `subject.binary/1` subject as leaf files (`REJECTED`); `V067` declares a collection scheme the verifier does not implement (`UNSUPPORTED`); `P001` and `P007` pin `subject_scope` (opaque input versus declared collection)
* feature:Schemas: `verification-package` — the closed member set of a package and the shape of its `commitment_inclusion_proof`; the positive packages validate and `V069`, `V070` fail it
* feature:Primitives: `collection-merkle/certificate-element.json` — element inclusion from the certificate and one leaf value alone (`verifier-toolkit/1` §8.4.2): signed certificates with a heterogeneous collection, a repeated value reported at every position, a text leaf matched after canonicalization, an absent value as a negative outcome, leaves that do not fold to the signed root, and a certificate that is not Issued-valid
* feature:Vectors: the effective-units binding of `capacity.commitment/1` §13.1.1 — `P008` declares three units for three subjects; `V041` declares one unit for three subjects and expects `INDEPENDENT_INVALID` (`EFFECTIVE_UNITS_BINDING_VALID`)
* feature:Primitives: 28 byte-contract families for the constructions underneath the verdicts — subject canonicalization (binary, text, email, collection, ai-decision, context-snapshot), strict JSON parsing, collection Merkle fold, original material, holder key sets, pseudonym, key derivation, issuer key derivation, deterministic ECDSA P-256, capacity evidence, level-1 binding, operator receipts, CCVP consistency, rules domain, submission sets and their limits, settlement preimages, artifact shape, manifest resolution, composite M′ framing, composite verification, independence-proof signatures and a full verification package. They bind issuer-side and settlement-side implementations as strictly as the fixtures bind verifiers, divided by role (see the root README)
* feature:Primitives: `ccvp-consistency` runs CCVP/1 over signed certificates — every target and snapshot is a CCSP/1 certificate of the conformance domain, written by the generator with deterministic keys and signatures. 31 cases pin the model, not only the arithmetic: snapshot issuers derived from the signature (`signer.id` plus composite key fingerprint) so a snapshot signed by another key under the same label is ignored (`namespace_impersonation_*`); `scope.subject_profiles`, roles, and an optional `kid` that narrows but never admits; timestamps compared as instants (`instant_not_string`, `respelled_tie_is_equivocation`); snapshots and targets that are not Issued-valid; a snapshot whose `subject.context-snapshot/1` commitment or timestamp is wrong cannot be skipped. The oracle recomputes every certificate's validity and every outcome independently
* feature:Primitives: the JSON subject families pin the precondition from both sides — `negative_cases` an implementation must refuse and `positive_cases` it must accept with their canonical bytes: booleans and nulls preserved under `subject.context-snapshot/1`; `subject.ai-decision/1` admits arrays only for its bounded `input` and `output` parts, rejects numbers, booleans and nulls, and pins its exact salted-commitment part shape, record-local ids, unique material nonces, profiles, MIME essences and Gregorian UTC timestamps
* feature:Primitives: `holder-keyset` pins trees of 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 operation keys and 17 full disclosures checked against a root: the out-of-range `leaf_index` that would fold as an alias, `tree_size` zero, integers given as strings, fractions or negatives, siblings in uppercase, non-hex, short, missing or extra, an additional or missing member and non-canonical base64 all fail; `tree_size_alias_valid` verifies with other bytes than the reference disclosure, because `tree_size` is not committed to by the root and a disclosure is never an identity. `collection-merkle` refuses a proof object with an additional member
* feature:Primitives: `artifact-shape`, `manifest-resolution` and `submission-set-limits` pin something other than bytes — the field SET of each signed preimage, a refusal, and the admission constants. An extra field in a signed preimage, or a key resolved that no other implementation would resolve, is a divergence no digest vector can detect, because the digest is correct for the object that was hashed
* feature:Primitives: `settlement-preimages` pins the leaf and batch-binding preimages a node produces, with the `SHA3-256(0x00 || …)` leaf rule and the digests JCS would have produced pinned as tripwires, cross-checked against `P001`'s own artifacts
* feature:Primitives: `issuer-key-derivation` pins the composite key an issuer signs base_certificates with, from its 32-byte master seed alone; the key id is a label and never enters the derivation. `ecdsa-p256-deterministic` pins the P-256 signature an issuer emits byte for byte (SHA3-256 prehash, RFC 6979 nonce with HMAC-SHA3-256, low-s, raw `r||s`); a verifier accepts any valid low-s signature and is not held to it
* feature:Primitives: Three goldens carry signatures produced by the reference producer implementation with test keys (hedged ML-DSA-65 + composite, so verified rather than reproduced), checked against pinned public keys; every synthetic identifier in them is neutral
* feature:Primitives: They exist because cross-language divergence never surfaces as a failed verdict — it surfaces as two implementations disagreeing about the same bytes, years later
* feature:Schemas: `settlement-material` — the wire form of a leaf's settlement material under `settlement.aggregated.xrpl/1` (closed objects, constants, quantity and identifier formats). Every positive material validates and every adversarial fixture whose material defect is a shape fails it (`V004`, `V005`, `V023`, `V024`, `V045`, `V050`, `V058`, `V060`, `V062`, `V063`); the rules a schema cannot express are listed in its `$comment`
* feature:Schemas: `verifier-request` and `verifier-result`, JSON Schema Draft 2020-12; `failed_check_ids` admits only the identifiers of `verifier-toolkit/1` §9.7; all 160 fixture requests validate against the input schema, including incomplete artifact sets without a base certificate, and the output schema is exercised from the other side by a verifier validating a real `VerifierResult`
* feature:Schemas: Every property carries a `description`; the boolean result fields are documented as independent AXES, not aliases of `result_code`; `production_verification` carries whether a `*_VALID` speaks for the published production domain or for another configuration, `attested_node` the node a proof attests, and `subject_scope` that proving a container is not proving its contents. The ML-DSA-65 component is normative: its failure fails `BASE_CERTIFICATE_AUTHENTIC`
* feature:Reproducibility: Regenerating leaves `fixtures/` byte-identical, which is the audit. Every signature scheme used by the generator is deterministic (Ed25519, FIPS 204 deterministic ML-DSA-65, RFC 6979 P-256 with HMAC-SHA3-256); see `generator/DETERMINISM.md`
* feature:Versioning: Adding a fixture is a MAJOR bump — a verifier conformant against one vector set is not automatically conformant against a larger one, and partial conformance is not a defined state
